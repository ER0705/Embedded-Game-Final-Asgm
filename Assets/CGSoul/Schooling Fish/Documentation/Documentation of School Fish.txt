School Fish


This project will help you if you need a school of fish that swim in an endless loop.


The Crowd of School Fish contains 30 low-poly fish.
All fish have a loop animation of 900 frames..


Technical details
Number of textures: 2
Texture dimensions: 512x512 px
Polygon count of one fish: 360 Tris
Maximum polygon count: 10.8K Tris
Number of meshes/prefabs: 30
Rigging: Yes
Animation count: 1
Animation type list: It is one animation loop duration 900 frames
UV mapping: Yes
